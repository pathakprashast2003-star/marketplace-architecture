Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Clear answers on our membership model, trial duration, and direct payment workflow.

How does the 90-day Founding Member free trial work? ↓

The first 100 approved creators receive a completely free 90-day VIP pass ($0.00 charged today). You get full storefront publishing, direct client inquiries, and verified status. If you choose to stay after 90 days, membership is a flat $49/mo. You can cancel with one click in your settings at any time before day 90 and never pay a cent.

Do you ever take a percentage cut of my client bookings? ↓

Never. Nexus operates on a pure directory subscription model. Whether you book a $1,500 day shoot or a $50,000 commercial campaign, you invoice clients directly using Stripe, QuickBooks, wire transfer, or check. Nexus takes exactly 0.0% of your earnings.

How do clients contact and pay me? ↓

Clients send inquiries directly through your storefront contact form or via your listed email and phone number. All messages are forwarded directly to your primary inbox with project scope, budget, and dates. You communicate and invoice on your own terms with no platform intermediary.

What are the requirements to be approved by Tyrone? ↓

Every applicant is manually evaluated by founder Tyrone Strickland Jr. We look for demonstrated commercial production experience, an active portfolio/reel, verified gear ownership or operator proficiency, and professional response discipline.

I'm a hiring client or agency producer. Does Nexus cost anything for me? ↓

Nexus is 100% free forever for clients, brands, and agencies. There are zero service fees, zero buyer markups, and no account is required to browse and contact talent. You negotiate terms directly with the creators.

Can I pause or cancel my membership at any time? ↓

Yes. You can cancel your subscription with 1 click directly in your billing settings anytime. If you take a full-time role or go on an extended production, you can also toggle "Vacation Mode" to pause inquiries without losing your storefront configuration.
