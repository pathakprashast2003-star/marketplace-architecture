How Creators Are Featured

The homepage Direct Roster features exactly 5 operators per weekly cycle. Creators earn placement via 5 objective production criteria:

1
Discipline Balance: Exactly 1 operator per core discipline (Cinema, FPV Drone, Illustration, Spatial 3D, Action Motion).
2
Tier-1 Brand Client Credits: Minimum 2 verified commercial credits with Fortune 500 or cultural brands (e.g. Nike, Porsche, Apple, Red Bull).
3
Hardware & Licensing Lock: Verified cinema-grade sensor (ARRI/RED/Inspire 3) and FAA Part 107 flight certification.
4
Sub-2h Guaranteed Response SLA: Maintained median inquiry response speed under 2 hours.
5
Weekly Performance Rotation: Rotated every Monday based on client inquiry velocity and 100% 5-star review rating.
