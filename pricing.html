FAQ

(EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW)
How does the 90-day Founding Member free trial work?

The first 100 approved creators get 90 days free with no credit card required upfront. You get a full verified profile, direct client inquiries, and custom storefront tools. If you decide to stay after 90 days, membership is a flat $49 a month. You can cancel anytime from your settings in one click.

Do you ever take a cut of my client bookings?

No, 0%. Nexus operates as an open talent directory, not an agency or escrow middleman. You invoice clients directly using whatever tools you already use—Stripe, QuickBooks, wire transfer, or check. We never take a percentage or hold your money.

How do clients contact and pay me?

Clients reach out through your storefront contact form, or directly via your email and phone if you choose to publish them. Inquiries land straight in your inbox with project dates, budget, and scope. You handle all communications, contracts, and payments on your own terms.

What are the requirements to be approved?

Every applicant is reviewed by our team before their profile goes live. We look for active commercial work, an updated reel, and dedicated production gear (or proven experience operating cinema camera packages like ARRI, RED, or Sony Cine).

I'm a hiring client or agency producer. Does Nexus cost anything for me?

Nexus is completely free for producers, brands, and agencies. There are no service fees, no markups on day rates, and no account required to browse and contact talent.

Can I pause or cancel my membership at any time?

Yes. You can cancel in one click from your billing settings anytime. If you're heading out on an extended production or taking time off, you can also toggle Vacation Mode to temporarily hide your profile from search without losing your storefront setup or reviews.
